# Sofiia Tretiak

Python & AI engineer with a medical informatics background. I build AI and data products for health, end to end: from EEG research and computer vision to a RAG system over national drug registers.

### → [sofiia-tretiak.vercel.app](https://sofiia-tretiak.vercel.app)

## Selected work

- **Hidden confounds in EEG emotion recognition**: research traineeship, Neuroengineering Dept., Karadeniz Technical University (2026). I showed that published accuracy on the FACED dataset mostly comes from recognising the videos, not the emotions.
- **PharmaRAG**: medicine questions answered only from official leaflets in six countries, with a source for every sentence. [Live demo](https://pharma-rag-nine.vercel.app) · [Code](https://github.com/TSofi/pharma-rag)
- **Buckwheat Sort**: a calm browser game about sorting buckwheat, installable on a phone. [Play](https://buckwheat-sort.vercel.app/?lang=en)
- **Career Shark**: a Duolingo-style career path app for IT beginners. 7th of ~50 teams at the BEST Hackathon (24h). [Code](https://github.com/TSofi/career_shark_frontend)
- **EyeSpy**: real-time emotion and fatigue detection from a webcam (ResNet18, MediaPipe). [Code](https://github.com/TSofi/Emotions-and-stress-detector)
- **Travel Planner API**: a FastAPI service for planning art-focused trips. [Code](https://github.com/TSofi/travel-planner-api)
